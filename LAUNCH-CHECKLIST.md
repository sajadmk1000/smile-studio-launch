# Launch checklist

- [ ] Clinic owner confirms name, phone, address, exact map pin, opening hours and offered treatments.
- [ ] Replace conceptual imagery with licensed authentic clinic photos if available; obtain consent for people and cases.
- [ ] Confirm doctor biographies and credentials before publishing them.
- [ ] Confirm and permission-check any public patient reviews before displaying them.
- [ ] Decide whether to replace WhatsApp-led enquiry with a real booking integration; no appointment is auto-confirmed today.
- [ ] Add analytics only with owner approval and a privacy review.
- [ ] Once the public domain exists, generate and submit a domain-specific sitemap; verify search metadata.
- [ ] Review copy with the clinic for medical accuracy and local compliance.
- [ ] Test call, WhatsApp, map directions and enquiry with the real clinic team.

# Design system — Dr. Ameen's Smile Studio

- **Visual thesis:** Quiet clinical editorial design, precise rather than ornamental. A tactile dental study object anchors the story; it is explicitly conceptual.
- **Emotional thesis:** Calm enough to ask questions, confident enough to make an enquiry.
- **Typography:** Cormorant Garamond for editorial display and DM Sans for body/interface. Large but not decorative-only. No fabricated handwritten medical annotations.
- **Colour:** Mineral off-white `--background`, botanical deep ink `--primary`, pale stone `--secondary`, muted leaf accents. All palette values live as semantic tokens in `src/styles.css` and include dark equivalents.
- **Photography:** Daylit still-life images with genuine material detail; no stock smiling patients, fake interiors or case studies.
- **Layout:** Restrained grid and generous margins; section rhythm alternates open editorial narrative, scannable treatment directory, dark visual feature and contact action.
- **Motion:** Only restrained hover transitions and native smooth in-page scrolling; reduced-motion override.
- **Signature moment:** A dark treatment-study diptych contrasts a close material study against oversized serif copy; the treatment directory reveals each decision as a full-width editorial line rather than a generic card.
- **Brand mark:** Temporary custom lowercase-a smile monogram, not presented as an official historic logo.
